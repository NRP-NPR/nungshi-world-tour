# Concert films

Three user-uploaded films are installed in filename order:

- `performance-01.mp4` — act one (45.5 seconds)
- `performance-02.mp4` — act two (81.9 seconds)
- `performance-03.mp4` — encore (206.7 seconds)

All files use H.264 video and AAC audio. The third film is portrait; the LED
screen preserves the entire frame using letterboxing, without cropping.

Native playback controls, play/pause and sound toggles are available. Audio
starts muted. Finishing a film advances to the next act; press Play film to
start the next performance. After the third film the award ceremony opens.
Visitors can also advance manually. Missing or replaced files retain the
artwork fallback and Retry video control, so the journey never gets blocked.

Replace these files at the same paths, then rebuild for production.
Optional: `tour-film.mp4` supplies the landing-page background; the campaign
image is used when it is absent or the visitor prefers reduced motion.
